🔧 Feature engineering
  • V3 → log1p(V3) or a rank/quantile transform; optionally cap at the 99th 
percentile
     V3 is heavily right-skewed (mean 1378.7 vs median 1000, max 12500, std 
1459.8) with 45 IQR outliers, which distorts linear models and split points on 
raw values.
  • V2 → log1p(V2) and/or winsorize the top values
     V2 is right-skewed (mean 5.5, median 4, max 50) with 45 IQR outliers and 
12 z-score outliers, so a monotone compression stabilizes the contribution of 
extreme donors.
  • V1 → Keep as-is; optionally bin into recency buckets (e.g., <3, 3–12, >12 
months) alongside the raw value
     V1 (recency) has only 7 outliers and zero 0.7% of the time, but the 
relationship between recency and return-to-donate is often non-monotone, so a 
bucketed version lets linear models capture a step effect.
  • V2, V4 → V2 / max(V4, 1) — donation frequency per month of observed tenure
     V2 is total donations and V4 is months since first donation; their ratio 
is a normalized intensity measure that is comparable across donors with 
different histories.
  • V3, V2 → V3 / V2 — average blood volume per donation
     V3 (total ccs, median 1000) divided by V2 (median 4 donations) yields a 
per-visit volume that removes the scale difference between frequent small 
donors and occasional large donors.
  • V1, V4 → V1 / max(V4, 1) and V4 - V1 (recency relative to tenure / active 
window)
     V4 (months since first donation, median 28) and V1 (months since last 
donation, median 7) together describe the donation window; their ratio 
contrasts lapsed donors against continuously active ones.

⚠ Pitfalls
  • 215 exact duplicate rows (28.7% of data); a random split leaks identical 
rows into train and test
     → Verify whether the duplicates are genuine repeat observations; if not, 
de-duplicate before splitting, and use GroupKFold on a donor identity key or 
deduplicated rows inside cross-validation.
  • Moderate class imbalance (Class 1 = 76.2%, Class 2 = 23.8%)
     → Use stratified CV and report AUC/PR-AUC plus per-class F1 rather than 
raw accuracy; consider class_weight='balanced' or scale_pos_weight instead of 
resampling on this small dataset.
  • Small sample (748 rows, 4 predictors); a single random split will have high
variance
     → Use repeated stratified k-fold (k=5 or 10) and report mean ± std across 
folds; avoid tuning on a single held-out split.
  • Heavy outliers/skew in V2 and V3 (45 IQR outliers each, V3 max 12.5x its 
median)
     → Winsorize or log1p-transform these columns, or rely on scale-invariant 
tree models; for linear models always fit the scaler inside each CV fold to 
avoid leakage.
  • Feature names V1–V4 are opaque, so it is easy to apply the wrong transform 
or misread importances
     → Map them to their documented meaning (recency, frequency, monetary/ccs, 
time since first donation) and sanity-check that coefficient signs and 
importances agree with domain expectations.
  • No missing values today, but the pipeline may silently coerce them later
     → Add explicit imputation/validation steps and assert dtypes (all features
are int64) before training so unseen data does not silently shift the 
distributions.
