     These columns have substantial right-skew and many outliers (e.g., 
V3/V4/V5/V6/V7), which can help linear models and stabilize training.
  • V1 → Treat as age-like numeric feature; also consider age bins or spline 
terms.
     V1 looks like a bounded continuous variable (4 to 90) and nonlinear age 
effects are common in clinical tabular data.
  • V8, V9, V10 → Keep as continuous predictors; try standardized versions and 
interaction terms among the liver-function markers.
     V8-V10 are comparatively compact numeric measures with less extreme skew 
than V3-V7, and correlated biomarker interactions may be informative.
  • V3 x V4, V5 x V6, V6 x V7 → Create ratio or product features where 
clinically sensible, such as V5/V6, V6/V7, and pairwise interactions among 
strongly related lab values.
     Several lab-style variables (V3-V7) vary over wide ranges; 
ratios/interactions can reveal relative imbalance not captured by raw values.
  • All numeric columns → Try missingness-free but robust preprocessing: 
median/robust scaling, tree-based models without scaling, and a linear-model 
pipeline with standardization.
     No missing values are present, but the mix of scales and outliers makes 
preprocessing choice important.

⚠ Pitfalls
  • Duplicate rows (13 exact duplicates, 2.2% of data)
     → Verify whether duplicates are genuine repeated measurements; if not, 
remove them before any split or use group-aware splitting to prevent leakage.
  • Class imbalance in Class (about 71% vs 29%)
     → Use stratified train/validation splits, consider class_weight='balanced'
or equivalent, and evaluate with imbalance-aware metrics such as ROC-AUC, 
PR-AUC, F1, and recall.
  • Heavy-tailed numeric features and many outliers in V3-V7
     → Use robust preprocessing, consider log transforms or winsorization, and 
compare linear vs tree-based models to avoid sensitivity to extreme values.
  • Potential data leakage from random splitting due to duplicates
     → Deduplicate before splitting or ensure duplicated records cannot appear 
in both train and test; keep a strict holdout set.
