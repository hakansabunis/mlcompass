⚠ Pitfalls
  • Duplicate rows are common (215 exact duplicates, 28.7% of the data).
     → Verify whether duplicates are genuine repeated observations; if not, 
deduplicate before modeling or use a split strategy that prevents identical 
rows from appearing in both train and test.
  • Potential train/test leakage from random splitting with duplicates.
     → Use group-aware splitting or deduplicate first; at minimum, check that 
no exact row repeats cross the split boundaries.
  • Class imbalance in Class (about 76% vs 24%).
     → Use imbalance-aware metrics and training settings such as class weights,
threshold tuning, or resampling; avoid relying on accuracy alone.
  • Small dataset size with only 748 rows.
     → Prefer cross-validation and simpler models first; keep the model search 
conservative to reduce overfitting.
  • Outliers in V1, V2, and especially V3 may overly influence some models.
     → Inspect extreme values, consider robust scaling or transformation, and 
compare performance with and without outlier handling.
