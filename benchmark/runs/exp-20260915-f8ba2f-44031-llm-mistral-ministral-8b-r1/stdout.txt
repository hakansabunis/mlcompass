🔧 Feature engineering
  • MedInc → Apply log transformation (e.g., `log1p(MedInc)`) or bin into 
quantiles.
     High skew (mean > std) and outliers (681 IQR outliers) suggest 
transformation to stabilize variance and improve model performance.
  • AveRooms → Create ratio features like `AveRoomsPerBedroom = AveRooms / 
AveBedrms` or `RoomsPerPopulation = AveRooms / Population`.
     Both columns show extreme outliers (133 Z-score outliers for `AveRooms`, 
1424 IQR outliers for `AveBedrms`), suggesting domain-specific relationships 
may exist.
  • ['Latitude', 'Longitude'] → Derive `distance_to_coast` (if coastal data is 
available) or `geographic_cluster` (e.g., K-means on lat/lon).
     Geospatial patterns (e.g., proximity to amenities) may correlate with 
`price`; raw lat/lon alone may not capture spatial effects.
  • price → Treat as censored data: model only values below the cap (1.79176) 
or use Tobit regression.
     4.7% of observations are piled at the max value, indicating potential 
measurement bias or censoring (per warning).

⚠ Pitfalls
  • Censored target (`price` capped at 1.79176)
     → Validate data collection process (e.g., survey limits) and consider 
censored regression methods or subsetting observations below the cap.
  • Outliers in `AveRooms`, `AveBedrms`, and `Population`
     → Use robust scaling (e.g., `RobustScaler`) or outlier-resistant models 
(e.g., Random Forest) to avoid undue influence on linear models.
  • No explicit train/validation split or feature leakage risk
     → Stratify splits by geographic regions (lat/lon) to avoid spatial 
autocorrelation bias.
