# A8: was it mlcompass, or was it just a second try?

**Run:** 2026-09-16 · **Experiment:** `ab-20260915-d52979`, 36 runs ·
**Scoring:** `ab-20260915-d52979-v2` (A7 + A11 rules) ·
**Seed 20260915, matching the 108-run battery** · **Evidence:** `runs/ab-20260915-d52979-*`

Yusuf Ünlü raised the objection that made this run necessary: `advise+audit`
differs from `control` in **two** ways at once. It is told what is wrong, and
it gets a second attempt. The 108-run battery could not say which one
produced the fall in defect count, so the result could not be attributed to
mlcompass.

This arm separates them. `control+revise` is the control prompt, then a
second turn saying only *review the script you just wrote and fix any
problems you find in it* — no findings, no checklist, no concern named. Same
budget as `advise+audit`; everything mlcompass removed.

---

## The answer

| Lane | `control` | **`control+revise`** | `advise` | `advise+audit` |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| deepseek-flash | 13 | 11 | 13 | **4** |
| mistral-ministral-8b | 8 | 6 | 3 | **0** |
| ollama-qwen2.5-7b | 6 | 9 | 4 | **1** |
| openai-gpt-5.4-mini | 6 | 9 | 6 | **3** |
| **total** | **33** | **35** | **26** | **8** |

**A second attempt on its own does nothing.** 33 → 35, marginally worse, two
lanes down and two up — noise around no effect. The same budget spent on
mlcompass's findings gives 8.

The within-arm view is sharper still. Across the 35 runs that produced a
defect count on both sides of the revision turn:

```
unchanged   33
worse        2
better       0
```

**Self-revision without mlcompass did not fix a single defect in 35 runs.**
The model was handed its own script and asked to find problems in it, and it
found none it could act on — while the same model, shown `mlcompass audit`
output on the same kind of script, fixes them.

This is the result the arm was built to produce, and it is the one that lets
the attribution be stated: **the fall from 33 to 8 is the findings, not the
turn.**

## Why this run exists twice

An earlier execution of this arm (`ab-20260915-19fa60`) gave the same answer
— 35 → 37 — on seeds 0/1/2, which are **three different splits**. The 108-run
battery used seed 20260915 for all three repetitions, one split. Comparing
scripts written against different `train.csv` files to scripts written
against one is not the comparison this arm is for, so it was rerun matched.

Recorded because the result favoured the hypothesis both times. A correction
that helps you deserves more scrutiny than one that does not, and the way to
give it that scrutiny is to remove the objection rather than argue it is
small.

## What this does not settle

- **`advise` is not isolated.** `control` → `advise` (33 → 26) and
  `control+revise` → `advise+audit` (35 → 8) are both available, but the
  arm that would separate the advice block from the audit block —
  `advise` plus a *content-free* second turn — has not run. What is
  established is that the turn is not doing the work; which of the two
  mlcompass channels is doing it remains open.
- **The held-out score.** 22 of 36 runs scored; 6 scripts failed to run, 1
  produced no revised script, and the rest saved artefacts the scorer cannot
  load (A6). The same shortfall as the main battery, and the same conclusion:
  no consistent effect on held-out score could be shown.
- **One run has no defect count at all** —
  `44031-control+revise-deepseek-flash-r1` — so the totals rest on 35 runs,
  not 36.
