\paragraph{Use of AI assistants}
The authors used Claude (Anthropic), principally Claude Opus 5.5 through Claude
Code, during 2026. Claude drafted and revised text in every section from the
authors' notes and results; wrote and tested most of the measurement harness and
analysis scripts and parts of the verification code; ran the batteries under the
authors' direction; checked bibliographic entries; simulated a multi-reviewer
pre-submission review; built the sheet and sampling script of the registered
blind audit, which the authors decided not to label; and wrote the scripts
through which model judges labeled the audit items. The models
\texttt{deepseek-chat}, \texttt{gpt-5.4-mini} and \texttt{qwen2.5:7b} are
subjects of the study (Section~\ref{sec:design}); two natural language inference
models and the LLMs \texttt{gpt-5.4-mini}, \texttt{deepseek-chat} and
\texttt{gpt-5.5} served as judges (Section~\ref{sec:rq1}). The authors chose the
research questions and design, reviewed and edited all text, inspected and tested
the code, recomputed the reported numbers from the released records, checked the
references, and made all analytical and interpretive decisions. Claude is not an
author, and the authors take full responsibility for the paper.
