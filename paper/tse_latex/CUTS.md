# What moved between the two builds

Two manuscripts, one study. `../emse_latex/` is the Springer `sn-jnl`
single-column version (43 pages, 17,957 words); this directory is the IEEE
two-column condensation (**14 pages, 11,794 words**).

The page numbers are not comparable, which is the reason both exist. Measured
on this repository's own builds:

| format | words per page |
|---|---|
| IEEE two-column (this build) | 842 |
| Springer `sn-jnl` (EMSE build) | 418 |

So 43 `sn-jnl` pages is about 20 IEEE pages of text. Getting to 14 took a real
26 % cut, not a reformat.

**No number changed.** Every rate, count, interval and table entry is identical
in both builds, and both are generated from the same replication package. If
they ever disagree, this file is the bug.

## Moved to the appendix

| Was | Now |
|---|---|
| §3.3 Proposition 2 and its proof (repair asymmetry) | Appendix A, with a one-paragraph summary left in §3 |
| §4 fail-closed semantics table (8 rows) | Appendix B |

Proposition 2 is the piece a reviewer already suggested demoting from a
proposition to a design lemma, on the grounds that two pages of
enforcement-theory argument were sitting in front of the empirical
contribution. Moving it is that suggestion taken.

## Kept, deliberately

Everything a reviewer would need to check the argument:

- All five RQs, with their tables.
- All four figures.
- The Guardrails swap (35.0 % vs 0/200) and its narrowing to a *default
  configuration*.
- The paraphrase decoupling, with the full six-variant table.
- The second-provider null, at full length, because it is the strongest
  argument in the paper for the position the paper takes.
- The differential audit and the correction that moved a result *against* our
  hypothesis (Table VI). This is the part most easily cut and the part least
  worth cutting.
- Every scope limit on the guarantee: free text, evidence-consistency vs
  correctness, and the ungoverned tool-server surface.

## Rebuilding

```bash
tectonic -X compile paper/tse_latex/main.tex --outdir paper/tse_latex
```

Figures 2 and 4 are generated by `fig_arms.py` and `fig_defects.py` in this
directory; Figures 1 and 3 are rasters shared with the `sn-jnl` build.

## Layout: what had to change for two columns

The first two-column build had four things running out of their column, which
is the failure a single-column draft hides. All four are fixed at the source
rather than by shrinking the page.

| Problem | Where | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Text ran 132pt past the paper's edge and was cut mid-word | p3, the set $F$ of reference-bearing fields as one unbreakable math group | broken into a displayed `array` |
| Table too wide by 77pt | Table I, seven arms | `table*`, full width |
| Table too wide | Table II, paraphrase sweep | `table*`, full width |
| Table too wide by 35pt | Table V, corrections | `ootnotesize`, two words shorter |

Two figures were drawn for a 4.9in measure and had been squeezed into a 3.5in
column, which left their axis labels and Figure 4's legend unreadable. Both are
now `figure*` at full width: Figure 3 (paraphrase sweep) and Figure 4 (per-rule
defects). Figures 1 and 2 survive at column width and were checked at print
scale.

Float parameters are loosened in the preamble. Eleven floats over fourteen
pages is dense, and the stock `	opfraction` / `	extfraction` values queued
them until a page top could take several at once, which produced one page
carrying four floats with the body text in fragments between them.

Verified on every build: zero overfull `\hbox`es, no text beyond the text
block, no undefined references.
